CCTV built around Logan's mix of housing

Logan's established suburbs, including Underwood, Slacks Creek, Marsden and parts of Loganholme, are mostly brick veneer homes with straightforward roof access, which makes a standard four-to-six camera layout (front door, back door, side gate, driveway) quick to run and reasonably priced. Further out in Park Ridge and Jimboomba, blocks get noticeably bigger, sometimes acreage, so the same camera count often needs to stretch further to cover a long driveway or a paddock boundary: the extra cost there is mainly cable length, not extra complexity.

In between, Springwood, Daisy Hill, Shailer Park and Rochedale South often sit on sloped blocks, where getting camera height and angle right matters more than on a flat block: a camera mounted at a standard height on a sloping driveway can end up aimed at the sky or the ground if it's not adjusted for the grade.

NVR sizing for Logan properties

A typical Logan home with four to six cameras and two to three weeks of footage retention runs comfortably on a mid-range NVR. Acreage properties with more cameras and longer retention expectations, or businesses in the Browns Plains and Crestmead industrial areas wanting a month or more of footage, need a larger drive from day one rather than an upgrade six months in.

CCTV in Logan FAQs

How many cameras does a typical Logan home need?+

Most established Logan homes end up with four to six cameras: front and back doors, side access, and the driveway. Larger blocks in Park Ridge or Jimboomba often add one or two more to cover a longer driveway or a side paddock.

Can you install CCTV on an acreage property?+

Yes. Acreage blocks around Jimboomba and Park Ridge are common jobs. The main difference is longer cable runs and cameras placed further apart to cover the extra ground, rather than a more complex system.

Do you install CCTV for Logan businesses?+

Yes, including the industrial and warehouse areas around Browns Plains, Crestmead and Loganholme, where jobs typically cover more entry points and a larger NVR for extended footage retention.
