FJ SECURITY SOLUTIONS: IMAGE GENERATION PROMPTS
==================================================

These prompts are for a FUTURE reshoot or AI-image regeneration once the
real FJ Security Solutions logo and final brand palette are available.
The site currently ships with the ORIGINAL photos from the live site
(backed up in the project root as *-original.webp, reprocessed into the
responsive WebP files actually used on the pages): these prompts are not
needed for launch.

Every prompt below shares one consistent lighting and colour-grade
instruction so the finished set reads as a single photoshoot rather than
seven unrelated stock images:

  SHARED STYLE: Natural late-afternoon Queensland daylight, warm but
  slightly desaturated colour grade (soft golden-hour warmth without
  looking artificially orange), realistic ambient shadows, shot on a
  35mm-equivalent lens at f/4-f/5.6 for natural depth of field. No
  visible competing brand names, logos, or model numbers on any
  equipment: generic dome/bullet camera shapes and unbranded alarm
  keypads only. Incorporate FJ Security Solutions brand colours (once
  the logo is supplied and the palette is sampled from it) into
  technician workwear, vehicle signage, or equipment accents where
  natural to the scene.

Keep filenames identical to what's already in the project root so a real
photo or regenerated image drops straight in with zero code changes.
