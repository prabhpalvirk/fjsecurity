Choosing the right mix

Services can work together rather than in isolation

Many customers are comparing more than one option. This page helps connect the services that are often discussed together instead of making important information harder to find.

Visibility and review

CCTV is useful when you want live visibility, recorded footage and app-based access to what is happening around the property.

Detection and alerts

Alarm systems suit customers who want a dedicated response to entry points and movement, whether the property is residential or commercial.

Connectivity and communication

Intercoms and data cabling help with access management and the reliable network performance needed by connected devices.

Yes. FJ Security Solutions offers CCTV, alarms, intercoms and WiFi/data cabling, so you can discuss a single service or a combined installation when you enquire.

Do you work on both homes and businesses?

Yes. The current business information applies across residential and commercial properties throughout Logan, Brisbane and the Gold Coast.

What should I send before asking for a quote?

Include the property location, which services you are comparing, and the areas or entry points you want covered. If remote viewing, app access or visitor communication matters, mention that as well.
