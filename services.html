Choosing the right mix

Services that work together, not in isolation

Most enquiries aren't for a single, isolated service. They're a combination, and it helps to think about what you're actually trying to achieve rather than picking a product off a list.

Visibility and review

CCTV suits anyone who wants live visibility, recorded footage and app-based access to what's happening around the property. It's the most requested service, and often the starting point for a broader system.

Detection and alerts

Alarm systems suit a dedicated response to entry points and movement, whether the property is residential or commercial, and pair naturally with CCTV so you know exactly what triggered an alert.

Connectivity and communication

Intercoms and data cabling handle access management and the reliable network performance that every other connected device depends on. It's often the quiet fix behind a system that "should" be working but isn't.

Can I request more than one service in the same quote?+

Yes. Most jobs actually combine two or more services, such as CCTV with data cabling, or an alarm with an intercom. Tell us everything you're considering and we'll quote it as one job.

Do you work on both homes and businesses?+

Yes, across all four services. Residential and commercial jobs are both a normal part of the workload, from a single home camera to a full warehouse fit-out.
