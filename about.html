About FJ Security Solutions

A locally owned and operated security installer, focused on reliable equipment, tidy work and straightforward advice, not a national call centre reading from a script.

Who we are

Focused on reliable systems and clear advice

FJ Security Solutions is locally owned and operated, licensed and insured, and works directly with the people who own the properties being secured, not through a national call centre or a rotating cast of subcontractors. When you call, you're speaking with the person actually planning and running the job.

The business covers four core services, CCTV, alarm systems, intercoms and WiFi/data cabling, for both families and businesses across Logan, Brisbane and the Gold Coast. Rather than pushing a fixed package, the approach is to look at the property, work out what coverage or protection actually matters there, and recommend equipment and placement that suits it.

Licensed and insured

Every job is carried out under the relevant security licensing and insurance cover, which matters both for the quality of the install and for your own peace of mind that the work has been done properly and is covered if something goes wrong.

What that looks like day to day

In practice, it means tidy cable runs instead of cameras wired in whatever way was quickest, equipment chosen to suit the site rather than whatever's cheapest to stock, and a walkthrough at the end of the job so you actually know how to use what's been installed, not just that it's technically switched on.
