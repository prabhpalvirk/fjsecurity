Suburbs we cover across Logan

Housing stock and what it means for installs

Older, established pockets, including Slacks Creek, parts of Loganholme, Marsden and Underwood, are mostly brick veneer homes built from the 1980s and 90s, usually with a roof cavity that's easy to access but little or no existing data cabling, so a CCTV or alarm job there is often a full new cable run rather than a top-up of something already in place. Further out, newer estates around Park Ridge, Jimboomba, Regents Park and parts of Crestmead tend to be larger blocks, sometimes acreage, with longer driveways and more perimeter to cover, which usually means more cameras and longer cable runs rather than more complex ones. In between, suburbs like Springwood, Daisy Hill, Shailer Park and Rochedale South mix both eras, often on sloped blocks where camera height and cable routing take a bit more planning.

What's most in demand in Logan

CCTV is the most requested service across Logan, largely because of how common long driveways and side-access gates are on typical Logan blocks. A single front-door camera rarely covers enough. Alarm systems are popular in the older, established suburbs where homes back onto reserve land or walkways. Intercoms come up most often on the larger blocks and gated estates around Park Ridge and Jimboomba, and data cabling work is common in newer estate homes that need an extra point or two the builder didn't include, as well as in older homes with no existing cabling at all.

Getting to you

Being based in Logan keeps travel time low across most of the area. Springwood, Underwood, Loganholme and Beenleigh are all a short trip from base, and jobs further out toward Jimboomba or Park Ridge are still well within the everyday service area, just factored into the quote as normal rather than treated as a special trip.
