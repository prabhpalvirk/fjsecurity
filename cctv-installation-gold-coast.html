CCTV Installation on the Gold Coast

Camera systems for Gold Coast homes and units, from Southport high-rise to new estates in Coomera and Pimpama.

CCTV built around the Gold Coast's growth

In Southport and parts of Nerang, high-rise and townhouse living means CCTV coverage is usually scoped to an individual unit's entry and balcony, with body corporate by-laws deciding what's possible on common property or the building facade. We check that before quoting rather than assuming a standard install applies.

Further north, Coomera, Upper Coomera, Pimpama and Ormeau are some of the fastest-growing suburbs in the state, and most new-build homes there go up with no security system at all: a full install from scratch is the norm, often with a longer driveway or a side-access point that an older, smaller block wouldn't have. Helensvale, Pacific Pines, Robina and Burleigh Heads sit in between, mixing established low-set homes with newer townhouse development, where a standard four-to-six camera layout usually covers it.

Gated estates and community entries

A lot of the Gold Coast's growth corridor sits inside master-planned or gated communities. The community entrance itself is normally managed by the estate, so our CCTV work focuses on your individual property and its own entry points, which we'll clarify with you upfront so there's no confusion about what's covered.

Why local knowledge matters here

Quoting a high-rise unit in Southport and a new-build home in Pimpama are genuinely different jobs. Knowing which body-corporate questions to ask upfront, or how far a growth-corridor driveway typically runs, keeps the quote accurate the first time.

Before install day on the Gold Coast

For a unit or apartment, check what your building's by-laws say about fixtures on common property before we quote, so the scope reflects what's actually approvable. For a new estate home, let us know if landscaping or fencing is still to be completed. It's often more efficient to run cable before a driveway or side path is finished than to work around it afterwards.

CCTV on the Gold Coast FAQs

Can CCTV be installed in a Gold Coast high-rise unit?+

In many cases yes, though building by-laws usually restrict what can go on common property or the building facade: coverage is typically scoped to your entry and balcony, with anything beyond that requiring body corporate sign-off.

How is CCTV different for a new estate home in Coomera or Pimpama?+

New estate homes usually go up with no existing security system, so it's a full install from scratch, often with a longer driveway or side-access point to cover compared to an older, smaller block.

Do you install CCTV in gated communities?+

Yes, though the community entrance itself is usually managed separately by the estate. We focus on covering your individual property and its entry points.
