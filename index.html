Frequently asked questions

Useful answers before you request a quote

What security systems does FJ Security Solutions install?+

CCTV camera systems, wired/wireless/hybrid alarm systems, door and gate intercoms, and WiFi/data cabling for homes and businesses across Logan, Brisbane and the Gold Coast.

Can I view my CCTV cameras remotely?+

Yes. Hikvision and Dahua systems are installed with mobile app access, so you can check live footage or review recordings stored on the NVR from your phone, wherever you are.

Do you install for both homes and businesses?+

Yes. Residential jobs range from a single camera on a townhouse to full coverage on a large block, and commercial jobs range from a small shopfront to a multi-camera warehouse setup.

What information do you need for a quote?+

Your property location, whether it's residential or commercial, the services you're after, and the entry points or areas you want covered. Photos of the property help too.
